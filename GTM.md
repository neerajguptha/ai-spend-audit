# GTM Strategy

## Target User

Early-stage startup founders (5–50 employees)
and Engineering Managers managing SaaS stacks.

---

## Where they are

- Indie Hackers
- r/startups
- r/SaaS
- YC Startup School groups
- X (Twitter) founders

---

## Acquisition Strategy

1. Post free audit tool on Reddit (value-first)
2. Cold outreach founders with "savings report"
3. Share viral audit links on X
4. SEO: "AI tool cost calculator"

---

## First 100 users

- 30 from Reddit posts
- 30 from Twitter threads
- 20 from direct founder DMs
- 20 from Product Hunt launch
